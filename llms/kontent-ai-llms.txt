# Kontent.ai

> Kontent.ai is the world’s first Agentic CMS and the only platform that helps organizations intelligently operate content at scale. By embedding AI agents into content workflows, Kontent.ai empowers organizations to automate operations, enforce governance, and accelerate time to market, without adding headcount.
Designed to accommodate even the most complex workflows without sacrificing speed, compliance, or results, Kontent.ai delivers a demonstrated ROI of 320%. Customers including WebMD Ignite, Elanco, PPG, and Oxford University benefit from a measurable step change in how their teams create, govern, and deliver content, driving greater operational efficiency, reducing risk, and accelerating content’s time to value.
Kontent.ai is a Microsoft partner and a vendor recognized by Gartner and Forrester. Learn more at [kontent.ai](https://kontent.ai/).


Key features and benefits:
- Kontent.ai delivers true omnichannel publishing, powering websites, mobile apps, chatbots, and digital experiences through its API-first architecture.
- Kontent.ai empowers developers with modern SDKs, REST & GraphQL APIs, and flexible integrations that fit seamlessly with frameworks like React, Vue, and Next.js.
- Kontent.ai scales globally with high-performance infrastructure, CDN delivery, and enterprise-ready uptime guarantees to handle large volumes of content and traffic.
- Kontent.ai provides an intuitive authoring interface with modular content blocks, real-time collaboration, and workflows designed for non-technical users.
- Kontent.ai ensures governance with versioning, rollback, role-based permissions, and approval workflows for enterprise-grade compliance.
- Kontent.ai integrates easily with analytics, marketing, personalization, commerce, and third-party systems, fitting into any digital ecosystem.
- Kontent.ai supports multilingual and multisite operations with built-in localization features and streamlined translation workflows.
- Kontent.ai offers transparent, flexible pricing models that adapt to enterprise needs while reducing total cost of ownership.
- Kontent.ai is enterprise-secure with SSO, encryption, API keys, audit logs, and compliance with global standards like GDPR and SOC 2.
- Kontent.ai provides world-class support with dedicated success managers, active community, and global partner ecosystem.
- Kontent.ai future-proofs digital experiences by embracing a composable architecture, ensuring flexibility and freedom from monolithic lock-in.
