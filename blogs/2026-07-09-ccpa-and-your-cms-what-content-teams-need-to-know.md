The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives California residents strong rights over how businesses collect and use their personal data. Kontent.ai is not currently in scope of the CCPA, but we treat personal data with the same care we apply everywhere else in the world. We are compliant with the GDPR, Australia's APPs, and applicable US privacy laws, and we are certified against ISO/IEC 27018, the global standard for protecting personal data in the cloud.
