Privacy in a content management system (CMS) is the set of controls, policies, and technical safeguards that protect the personal data of everyone who uses the platform, from the marketers logging in every day to the end users consuming the content. If you're a content creator, content manager, or marketer, this guide explains what privacy means in practice inside Kontent.ai and why it matters for your work.
